**ForgeTrail** is a persistent development system for building software with AI agents.

**Who it is for:** builders using Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, or similar agents who want structure without a heavyweight process.

**Why it works:** phases with exit criteria, a ledger in `appledger/`, and templates pre-loaded with production lessons. Each project leaves decisions, lessons, and a session handoff that future work follows.

## What it is not

- Not a project tracker, audit log, or observability product
- Not a hosted SaaS or a plugin
- Not a substitute for your judgment at phase gates

## Works alongside

ForgeTrail supplies the development method and uses [AppLedger](https://appledger.dev) for the project record. Other tools on the [Catalyst Forge shelf](https://catalystforge.com/tools/) cover publishing Markdown sites (FilePress), managing named local ports (LocalSlip), running and shipping local repositories (LocalHelm), and reviewing the work. Those companion tools are optional.
